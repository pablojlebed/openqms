# Appendix 1 — Item Receipt and Condition Record

**Parent procedure:** [13 – Handling of Items Received for Testing](./)

## Purpose

To document the receipt and initial examination of each item (or batch of items received together), and to record any anomaly identified and the decision taken. This record is the laboratory's evidence that an item's condition at the point it entered the laboratory's custody was checked and, where relevant, communicated to the customer.

## Instructions

1. **Reception personnel** complete Part A and Part B at the time of receipt — not retrospectively.
2. If an anomaly is identified, complete Part C, including the decision taken and who took it.
3. Part D is completed by the **Technical Manager** or **Quality Manager** if the item is placed in quarantine or if customer consultation is required.
4. The completed record is filed per [Procedure 00 — Document and Record Control](../00-document-record-control/index.md).
